1 · Pitch

The 30-second pitch

“Every morning, skilled workers across Mumbai stand at roadside nakas hoping someone picks them. Whether a family eats that night depends on who walks past.

RozGhar puts that naka on their phone. Work nearby with the pay, hours and pay day shown up front. A safety check that flags scams. A Kaam Card instead of a résumé. A pakka agreement both sides keep. And their rights and welfare schemes in plain Hindi or Marathi.

It works by voice, in three languages, and even on a ₹1,000 button phone by SMS. We didn't build another job portal. We built for irregular work and low digital literacy.”

The name. RozGhar = roz (daily) + ghar (home), and it sounds like rozgaar, employment. The logo is the day's sun rising over a home: a day's work that keeps a household going.

3 · Running it

How to run it on presentation day

  1. Open a terminal in the project folder: Documents\Naka.
  2. First time only: npm.cmd install
  3. For the smoothest demo, run the production build: npm.cmd run build then npm.cmd start. (For development: npm.cmd run dev.) In Command Prompt you can drop the .cmd.
  4. Open http://localhost:3000 in Chrome or Edge. Voice features work best there.
  5. Before presenting, open /demo and press Reset demo. Demo data lives in the browser (localStorage), so a reset returns everyone to the starting state.
Code on GitHub: github.com/Rohan0720surana/naka — the redesign is on branch rozghar-redesign; main has the first version.
Tip: keep the internet on. Fonts load from Google Fonts and voice recognition needs it; everything else runs locally.

4 · Demo script

The five-minute demo

Run this on /demo on a laptop. The left device is Ramesh's phone, the right window is Priya's employer site. Anything done on one side appears on the other instantly.

  1. Set the scene (30 s). On the landing page, scroll through “one morning at the naka”: the clock moves, the crowd thins, a car picks three men with nothing written down.
    “This is the problem. Work depends on luck, skill is invisible, terms are verbal, rights are far away.”
  2. Post work as Priya (45 s). In the employer window: Post work → Painter → Next → Next → choose Same day pay → Publish. Point at the live preview switching between हिंदी / मराठी / English and the safety check that stays at “Be careful” until a pay day is chosen.
    “Employers can't post vague work. Pay, hours and pay day are required.”
  3. Ramesh gets it (60 s). On the phone an alert drops in, in his language. Open it: big pay, hours bar, map, what's given, fair-pay meter, safety check. Tap Listen to hear it read aloud. Tap I want this work → the deal sheet → Yes, send.
  4. Make it pakka (40 s). Employer dashboard → Confirm. Both screens show the same agreement code and the PAKKA stamp lands.
    “Both sides hold the same agreement. If pay doesn't come, this is proof.”
  5. Pay and log (40 s). Employer: Mark work done & paid. Ramesh: Yes, I got it → coins drop into his Kamai Khata, and his days move toward the welfare board's 90.
  6. No smartphone (45 s). Switch the left device to Arjun (SMS). Show the Hindi job alert and press 1. A “Pakka!” SMS arrives.
    “We didn't leave out the people who most need this.”
  7. Close with a scam (30 s). On the worker app open the “Painters needed in Dubai” job: red banner, hazard tape, “Asks you to pay ₹15,000 first”. Switch the language to हिंदी to finish.

9 · Design

Design decisions

Typography

Khand condensed signboard headings in both scripts · Atkinson Hyperlegible body text, made for low-vision readers · Hind for Hindi/Marathi · Yatra One Hindi poster lines · Kalam handwriting for people's own words · Courier Prime typewriter for agreements.

Colour

Ink
Marigold
Leaf
Bus red
Paper

Ink for trust, marigold for action, leaf for fair/verified, red only for warnings and decoration. Every status uses colour and an icon and a word.

Motion with meaning

The naka fills up when you're free; a rubber stamp seals a deal; hazard tape slaps across a scam; coins drop into the Khata; the story's clock moves as you scroll; a BEST bus crosses the skyline. All motion respects “reduce motion” settings, and the reel can be paused.

Inclusion

Voice in and voice out; pictures before words; three languages; 44–48 px touch targets; strong contrast for sunlight; bigger-text switch; nothing hidden behind hover; keyboard focus rings; screen-reader labels; works on a 360 px phone.

10 · Under the hood

How it's built

PartChoice and why
FrameworkNext.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript. Builds as a static-first site, deployable to Vercel.
StylingTailwind CSS 4 with design tokens (colours, shadows, fonts) in one stylesheet.
MotionFramer Motion for every animation; Magic UI Marquee and Number Ticker components.
Shared demo stateZustand store persisted to localStorage. Separate frames (worker phone, employer site, SMS phone) stay in sync through storage events: that's how the two-sided demo works with no backend.
LanguagesA typed dictionary of ~300 strings in English, Hindi and Marathi; job text is generated from structured fields, so new jobs appear in every language.
VoiceBrowser Web Speech API: speech synthesis for read-aloud (Marathi falls back to a Hindi voice) and speech recognition for onboarding, with a parser for trade, years (digits or number words in 3 languages) and naka.
Safety checkRule-based: advance fee, kept documents, pay far above or below the usual range for the trade and unit, unclear pay day, unverified phone, vague location, jobs abroad.
Fair-pay meterUsual day rates per trade (sample Mumbai data), converted for per-job and monthly pay.
ArtAll illustrations (portraits, naka scenes, trade pictograms, bus, stamp) are hand-written SVG: crisp, tiny and themeable.
11 · Q&A prep

Questions judges are likely to ask

How is this different from a job portal like Apna or Naukri?
No résumé, no typing, no English required. It starts from the naka people already use, adds clear terms and scam protection, and keeps a record (agreement + Khata) that helps with pay disputes and welfare registration. It even works without a smartphone.
How do workers with low literacy use it?
Language is chosen first; onboarding is by voice or pictures; every job, agreement and scheme can be read aloud; trades are pictograms; numbers are big; there's a bigger-text switch.
What about people without smartphones?
SMS in Hindi with one-key replies (1 yes, 2 no, 0 help), and the Naka Saathi who registers and represents them. Show it on /demo → Arjun.
How do you detect scams?
A transparent rule set, explained in words: money asked from the worker, documents kept, pay several times the local rate, unclear pay day, unverified phone, vague address, jobs abroad (check eMigrate). Employers see the same check while posting.
How would you verify employers and workers in a real launch?
Phone OTP for both, masked calling, ratings tied to completed agreements, and vouches from Naka Saathis who know people personally. Aadhaar-based e-Shram linking could be optional.
Where does the data come from? Is there a backend?
PS2 allows mock data; this is a frontend prototype. All people, jobs and centres are sample data; the helpline numbers are real. The state model (jobs, agreements, Khata, SMS) is ready to be backed by an API.
Why should a worker trust the pakka agreement?
Both parties see identical terms before agreeing, it's timestamped with a code, shareable on WhatsApp, and the payment is confirmed by the worker. If pay doesn't arrive it becomes evidence for legal aid (15100).
How does it scale beyond Mumbai?
Nakas, areas and rates are data. Adding a city means adding its nakas and rate ranges; adding a language means one more dictionary column (Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati are marked “soon”).
Is it accessible?
Semantic HTML, labelled controls, visible focus, 4.5:1 contrast, reduced-motion support, screen-reader-only text for visual-only elements, and no horizontal scrolling on phones.
12 · Limits

Honest limits and what comes next

  • Prototype on sample data; no server, payments or real OTP.
  • Voice recognition depends on the browser (best in Chrome/Edge) and needs internet; read-aloud works offline in most browsers.
  • Hindi and Marathi text should be reviewed by native speakers; scheme amounts should be checked against official sources before launch.
  • Next: a real backend and SMS gateway (e.g. via a telecom API), employer verification, UPI payment confirmation, more languages, an IVR line for people who can't read SMS, and a pilot at one naka with a Saathi.
